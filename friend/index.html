📖 how to play

🎯 goal

  • Guide the butterfly to land on flowers.
  • Each flower collected = points.
  • Avoid the spider webs — 3 hits and it's over!

🎮 controls

  • move mouse / finger — butterfly follows
  • ← → ↑ ↓ arrow keys
  • space — flap (speed boost)

⭐ scoring

  • Each flower = +10 points
  • Chain flowers quickly = combo bonus
  • Combo multiplier = +5 × combo
  • Golden flowers = +25 bonus

⚠️ hazards

  • Spider webs drift slowly across the screen.
  • They get faster the longer you survive.
  • Webs pulse — watch for the glow.
✨ pro tip: fly through flowers in a smooth arc to keep your combo alive. The butterfly is fastest when you lead it gently!
